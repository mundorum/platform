Mundorum

An umbrella for environments that author and play interactive compositions: narratives, scenes of connected components, and clinical activities guided by LLM agents.

  • gnō- gno/

    A narrative language and its editor. Authors write an interactive story as a Markdown-based .gno text: scenes grouped into scenarios, entities that take the stage, dialogs, states and diverts that lead from one scene to another. The editor shows the story as a graph and plays it in a Player, optionally over a graphic presentation drawn as SVG. It runs entirely in the browser and can be used online.

    gnō- documentation and editor

  • noid noid/

    Composes and executes n-o-id scenes: directed graphs of Python components connected by publish/subscribe edges. Scenes are authored on the platform's shared server and run on a separate processing machine, the two communicating over HTTPS.

    noid documentation

  • VP Studio vp/

    Lets educators author virtual patient activities: a scripted sequence of steps in which an LLM tutor, and optionally an LLM patient, guides a student through clinical cases. Students play the activity in a chat-style player; a case can also be exported as a gnō- narrative.
